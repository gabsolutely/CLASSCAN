## Form 1: Criteria 1 — Comparison of CLASSCAN to Traditional Manual Headcount Method

**Directions:** Please read each statement carefully before answering. Select the response that best represents your assessment or experience with the CLASSCAN device. There are no right or wrong answers. Kindly answer all items honestly and completely. Your responses will be kept confidential and will be used solely for the purposes of this research study.

### Rating Scale

| Rating Scale | Qualitative Description |
|:---:|---|
| **5** | Highly Significant |
| **4** | Fairly Significant |
| **3** | Moderately Significant |
| **2** | Slightly Significant |
| **1** | Insignificant |

### Observation Items

#### a. Efficiency
1. CLASSCAN provides the classroom headcount in less time than the traditional manual method.
2. CLASSCAN provides real-time headcount information compared with manual counting.

#### b. Accuracy
1. CLASSCAN produces a headcount that matches the actual number of students.
2. CLASSCAN produces fewer counting errors than the traditional manual method.

#### c. Reliability
1. CLASSCAN provides consistent headcount results during repeated observations.
2. CLASSCAN maintains dependable count stability throughout continuous classroom sessions.

#### d. Convenience
1. CLASSCAN requires less effort to obtain the classroom headcount than the traditional manual method.
2. CLASSCAN makes classroom headcount monitoring more convenient than manual counting.

### Observation Items

#### a. Functionality
1. CLASSCAN successfully detects students inside the classroom.
2. CLASSCAN automatically counts the detected students.
3. The **LCD display** shows the current classroom headcount.

#### b. Accuracy
1. The displayed headcount matches the actual number of students present.
2. CLASSCAN minimizes errors when detecting and counting students.

#### c. Reliability
1. CLASSCAN provides consistent headcount results during repeated observations.
2. CLASSCAN maintains consistent performance across sequential quadrant scan cycles.

#### d. Precision
1. CLASSCAN provides a precise headcount when multiple students are present.

### Rating Scale

| Rating Scale | Qualitative Description |
|:---:|---|
| **5** | Highly Observed |
| **4** | Fairly Observed |
| **3** | Moderately Observed |

### Observation Items

#### a. Lighting Conditions
1. CLASSCAN successfully detects and counts students under different lighting conditions.
2. CLASSCAN maintains accurate headcount information when lighting conditions change.

#### b. Camera Positioning
1. CLASSCAN successfully detects and counts students when the camera is positioned at different locations.
2. CLASSCAN maintains proper detection when the camera angle is adjusted.

#### c. Student Capacity
1. CLASSCAN accurately detects and counts students when the number of students changes.
2. CLASSCAN continues to provide headcount information when the classroom has a larger number of students.
